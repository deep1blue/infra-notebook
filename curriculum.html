Staff AI/ML Interview Curriculum

The full map for a Staff AI/ML engineer loop — from your own background to pretraining, post-training, inference engineering, and the system-design rounds. The morning and evening lessons walk this map node by node; each lesson links back here, and each node below links forward to its lesson as it's written. The midday slot walks a separate LeetCode round map.

Phase 0 — Your resume, translated to LLM-staff fluency

You don't need to hide a classical-ML and leadership background. You need a translation layer: every strength you already have, restated in LLM vocabulary.

Classical ML → LLM translation layer Planned

Loss design and regularization are the same muscles as KL penalties and reward shaping; bias–variance thinking becomes scaling-law reasoning. Answers "your background is classical ML — how does it carry over to training LLMs?"

Bayesian roots → reward modeling & uncertainty Planned

Your Bayesian publications are directly relevant: reward models are learned preference distributions, and calibrated uncertainty over LLM outputs is a live Staff-level problem. Answers "how do you quantify uncertainty in a model's outputs?"

Experimentation & metrics → eval harnesses Planned

The cost-minimization triage work is the same instinct as eval-harness design and guardrail metrics: decide what "better" means before you optimize. Answers "how do you know a new model is actually better?"

Leading 4× growth → Staff narratives Planned

TikTok Live leadership — strategy, roadmapping, hiring, VP-level reporting — is the raw material for the behavioral bar and the system-design rounds. Answers "tell me about a hard technical decision you drove."

Phase 1 — Pretraining at scale

What happens before the model knows anything, and why the infra looks the way it does.

Tokenization & data pipelines Planned

BPE, packing, dataloaders — the unglamorous plumbing that decides throughput before a single flop. Staff angle: "why is your training job data-starved?"

Scaling laws (Chinchilla) Planned

Compute-optimal training as a budgeting tool: how many tokens per parameter, and what it costs to be wrong. Staff angle: "how would you size a pretraining run?"

Optimizers: AdamW & Muon Planned

Why AdamW is the default, where Muon changes the memory math, and what the optimizer state costs you at scale. Staff angle: "what dominates memory in your trainer?"

Mixed precision: FP16 / BF16 / FP8 Planned

Loss scaling, underflow, and why FP8 is a numerics project before it's a speedup. Staff angle: "your FP8 run diverged — walk me through the debug."

Distributed training: the four parallelisms + the fabric Done

Ring all-reduce, ZeRO/FSDP sharding, tensor and pipeline parallelism, and the NVLink/InfiniBand/NCCL fabric underneath it all. Staff angle: "where does scaling efficiency actually go?"

Large-batch training & LR scaling Planned

Gradient accumulation, the linear scaling rule and its limits, and batch-size effects on generalization. Staff angle: "how big can you push the batch before it hurts?"

Activation memory: gradient checkpointing Planned

Trading recompute for memory — the 30% rule of thumb and when it breaks. Staff angle: "you're OOM at 128k context — what do you change first?"

FSDP2 & async tensor parallelism Planned

Modern torchtitan patterns: DTensor sharding and overlapping comms with compute. Staff angle: "how do you hide all-reduce behind matmuls?"

Fault tolerance: checkpointing & elastic training Planned

Checkpoint cadence math, failure rates at 10k GPUs, and restarting without losing the run. Staff angle: "your cluster loses a node a day — design the recovery."

Phase 2 — Post-training: SFT → RL

Turning a document-completer into an assistant, then into something that reasons.

SFT mechanics: instruction tuning, chat templates, loss masking Done

Loss only on response tokens; the template must match at inference. Staff angle: "why did fine-tuning make your model worse?"

SFT data: curation, quality vs quantity, decontamination Planned

LIMA's lesson, data mixing, and why contaminated evals lie to you. Staff angle: "how do you build a fine-tuning dataset you trust?"

RLHF pipeline: SFT → reward model → policy optimization Planned

The three-stage pipeline and why each stage exists. Staff angle: "why can't you RLHF straight from the base model?"

Reward modeling: Bradley–Terry, reward hacking Planned

Preference data → scalar reward; Goodhart's law as an engineering problem. Staff angle: "your reward model is being gamed — how do you tell?"

DPO & ORPO: the RL-free derivation Planned

The closed-form optimal policy trick that skips the RL entirely. Staff angle: "when would you choose DPO over PPO?"

PPO: clipped objective, KL penalty, value head Planned

The trust-region intuition, why the KL term matters, and PPO's failure modes on LLMs. Staff angle: "why is PPO so finicky for language models?"

GRPO & RLOO: group baselines Planned

Dropping the value head: baselines from the rollout group itself. Staff angle: "what does the value network buy you, and what does it cost?"

RLVR: verifiable rewards & reasoning Planned

When the reward is checkable (math, code), RL teaches reasoning — not just style. Staff angle: "why did RL on verifiable tasks unlock reasoning?"

RL infra: rollout workers, async generation, vLLM in the loop Planned

Generation-bound training: the serving system inside the training loop. Staff angle: "your RL run is bottlenecked on rollouts — fix it."

Phase 3 — Inference engineering

Serving tokens cheaply and fast: where Meta-scale systems thinking lives.

KV cache Done

The memory tax on every generated token — and the first thing to optimize. Staff angle: "how much memory does a 128k-context request need?"

Continuous batching Done

Iteration-level scheduling: why static batching wastes GPUs. Staff angle: "how do you keep utilization high with mixed request lengths?"

Paged attention & prefix caching Planned

Virtual memory for KV cache; sharing prefixes across requests. Staff angle: "how do you serve a million near-identical system prompts?"

Quantization for serving Done

INT8/INT4 weights and KV cache: what breaks and how to measure it. Staff angle: "your quantized model regressed 2% — ship it?"

Speculative decoding Planned

Draft-then-verify: free latency when the small model agrees. Staff angle: "when does speculation stop paying off?"

Disaggregated prefill / decode Planned

Splitting the two phases onto different hardware for independent scaling. Staff angle: "TTFT and TPOT have different SLOs — design for both."

MoE inference: expert parallelism in serving Planned

Sparse activation means sparse memory traffic — and brutal load-balancing problems. Staff angle: "one expert is hot — what happens to tail latency?"

Serving stacks: vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM Planned

What each stack optimizes for and how to choose. Staff angle: "pick a serving stack for a new product and defend it."

Latency math: TTFT, tokens/sec, roofline, cost per token Planned

Back-of-envelope serving arithmetic — the single most deployable Staff skill. Staff angle: "how many GPUs do we need for 10k requests per second?"

Phase 4 — Architecture & efficiency

Why the transformer looks the way it does in 2026.

Attention variants: MHA → GQA → MLA Planned

KV-cache compression through architecture: fewer heads, latent compression. Staff angle: "why did everyone move to GQA?"

FlashAttention Planned

Attention is memory-bound, not compute-bound — tiling changes the asymptotics in practice. Staff angle: "why is naive attention slow even though it's 'just' O(n²)?"

MoE: routing, expert parallelism, load balancing Planned

Capacity without proportional compute; the all-to-all tax. Staff angle: "when does MoE beat dense at fixed inference budget?"

RoPE & positional encodings Planned

Relative positions via rotation; extrapolation to long context. Staff angle: "your model degrades past its training length — why?"

Phase 5 — Data & evaluation

Garbage in, garbage out — at trillion-token scale, with receipts.

Dedup & decontamination Planned

Near-dup detection at web scale; keeping benchmarks out of training. Staff angle: "your eval scores jumped 5 points — is it real?"

Data mixing Planned

Domain weights as hyperparameters; the ablations nobody can afford to rerun. Staff angle: "how do you decide the code-to-web ratio?"

Eval harnesses Planned

Reproducible evals, statistical significance, and eval-driven development. Staff angle: "design an eval suite for a new capability."

LLM-as-judge, human eval, red-teaming Planned

When automated judges work, when only humans do, and adversarial probing. Staff angle: "your judge agrees with itself but not with users — now what?"

Phase 6 — Staff system design & behavioral

The rounds that decide Staff: design at scale, own the tradeoffs, tell the story.

Design training on 10k GPUs Planned

Full-stack design: parallelism strategy, fabric, checkpointing, failure budget. The capstone of Phases 1–2.

Design inference for 100M DAU Planned

Capacity math, SLO tiers, batching, caching, cost per token. The capstone of Phase 3.

Cost & capacity modeling Planned

Translating architecture choices into dollars and GPUs — the Staff superpower. Answers "is this model worth serving?"

Reliability & incidents at scale Planned

Silent data corruption, stragglers, gray failures — and the postmortem culture. Answers "tell me about a production incident you owned."

Behavioral: hard technical decisions Planned

STAR-format stories from TikTok Live leadership: ambiguous calls, tradeoffs, outcomes.

Behavioral: technical strategy & influence Planned

Roadmapping, VP-level reporting, aligning teams without authority — your documented strengths, rehearsed.

Behavioral: mentoring & disagreement Planned

Growing engineers 4×, and the stories where you were wrong or overruled — interviewers probe for both.
